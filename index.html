The problem

Most property calculators are broken by design.

They ignore local tax law. They use round numbers that don't reflect reality. They stop at gross yield without telling you what actually lands in your pocket. And the good ones — the ones built by accountants or mortgage brokers — are buried behind consultations or subscription fees.

MMDAI was built to fix that. Real formulas. Real tax logic. Real local context. Free, in the browser, without asking for your email address.

The origin

Calculators built for personal use first.

The person behind MMDAI has spent over 15 years working across project management, operations, and investment — building systems, running businesses, and making the kind of financial decisions that require tools that actually work.

When evaluating property investments across different European markets, the available tools kept falling short. SDLT with the BTL surcharge. Section 24 vs limited company. Grunderwerbsteuer across 16 Bundesländer. LMNP réel vs micro-BIC. No single tool handled any of these correctly — let alone all of them.

So they were built from scratch. Then made public. Then expanded to cover more markets, more edge cases, more of the real complexity that investors actually face.

The commitment

Maintained, updated, and honest about limitations.

Tax law changes. SDLT thresholds shift. Bundesland rates get updated. LF 2025 rewrote the LMNP exit calculation overnight. Every calculator on this site is reviewed and updated when the underlying rules change — not left to go stale.

Every calculator includes a disclaimer because these are tools for informed decision-making, not substitutes for professional advice. The goal is to help you arrive at the conversation with your accountant or broker already knowing the right questions to ask.

Principles

How every calculator is built.

01 — Accuracy first

Real tax law, not approximations.

Every calculator uses the actual legislative source — HMRC, GNotKG, HUD, Code Général des Impôts. Rates are verified against official sources and updated when legislation changes.

02 — Local depth

Localization is not translation.

A UK BTL calculator needs Section 24 and ICR stress tests. A German one needs AfA by Baujahr and Bestellerprinzip. A French one needs 6 régimes fiscaux. Surface-level localization is not enough.

03 — No paywalls

Free to use. Always.

The web calculators are free, without signup or email capture. Excel versions with deeper analysis are available for those who want offline, editable models. The core tool is never locked.

04 — Honest limits

Tools, not advice.

Every calculator includes a clear disclaimer. These tools help you understand the numbers — they don't replace an accountant, mortgage broker, or solicitor. The goal is to help you ask better questions.

05 — Maintained

Updated when the law changes.

Tax law is not static. SDLT changed in April 2025. LF 2025 rewrote LMNP exit taxation. Sachsen raised Grunderwerbsteuer in 2023. Every page is reviewed against current legislation.

06 — No ads on decisions

Referrals are labelled. Always.

Some calculators include affiliate links to mortgage comparison tools. These are clearly labelled. MMDAI does not receive payment to skew calculation results or recommendations.
